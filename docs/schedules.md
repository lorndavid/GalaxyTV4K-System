# Schedule & Shift Management

## Overview
Administrators can configure multiple work schedules, working hours, lunch break windows, and grace periods.

### Features:
- **Configurable Work Days**: Toggle active working days (e.g. Monday–Friday, Monday–Saturday).
- **Shift Windows**: Defined `startTime` (e.g. `08:00`) and `endTime` (e.g. `17:00`).
- **Lunch Breaks**: Defined `breakStartTime` and `breakEndTime` deducted from total worked minutes.
- **Grace Periods**: Configured in Company Settings (e.g. 10 minutes grace before being marked late).
- **Default Schedule**: Automatically assigned to newly created employees.
